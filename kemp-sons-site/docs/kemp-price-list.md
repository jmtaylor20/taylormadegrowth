# Kemp & Sons price list (Nov 16, 2026)

Kemp's handwritten list, typed up. The live prices are whatever is on the
Prices page (/staff/prices), which Kemp's staff can change at any time. This
file is the starting point for formats that have no template on the site yet:
when a template is added, its Shopify product starts at these prices and joins
its price group in `netlify/lib/price-groups.ts`.

Prices include shipping (free shipping is advertised; Overnight is extra).
Gloss and matte are the same price. Questions: Austin, 334-707-2173.
